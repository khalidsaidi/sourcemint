SourceMint (SRCMNT) is an experiment in accounting for open-source contribution on-chain: a fixed-supply ERC-20 with no ICO and no token sale. Supply enters circulation as contributor rewards, every treasury movement is ledgered and code-verified — and the token trades freely.

Mission

Make contribution count, on-chain

Open source runs on work that is rarely visible and almost never accounted for. SourceMint is a small, honest experiment against that: a token whose only distribution channel is contribution to the project itself, with every movement recorded, reasoned, and machine-verified.

Earned first, traded freely

No ICO, no airdrop. Supply enters circulation as rewards to people whose contributions were merged, under a public reward policy. Once out, SRCMNT is an ordinary ERC-20 — holders trade it as they wish, and any treasury liquidity provision is ledgered like every other movement.

Accounted, provably

Every transfer out of a project wallet must appear in a public ledger with its reason. CI re-checks the ledger against the chain — an unaccounted movement fails the build in public.

Trust-minimized by design

No owner, no admin, no pause, no fees, no minting after deployment. Supply can only shrink, and only when a holder chooses to burn.

The treasury and rewards wallets are project-controlled; the split was funded in one on-chain transaction on 2026-08-27. On 2026-08-28 the treasury seeded a public ETH/SRCMNT pool on Aerodrome with 10,000 SRCMNT and 0.012 ETH, so rewards can be exchanged rather than being symbolic. The pool is permissionless: anyone can trade against it or add liquidity, and its price is set by the market, not by the project.

Don't take our word for it

The accounting invariant — every token leaving a project wallet has a ledgered reason — is enforced by code, not promises. The ledger lists each movement with its transaction hash and reason; the Ledger check replays the chain against it on every change and weekly. Verify it yourself from a clone of the repository:

python3 script/verify_ledger.py
